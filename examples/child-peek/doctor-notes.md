## Balance and humour

Emotional line: calm (b1) → anticipation (b2) → hope (b3) → letdown (b4) →
delight (b5). One small low before the ending, so the delight is earned. The
humour is a single reversal of expectation, landed by understatement; the
tone stays warm, so no further jokes were added.

## Summary

1. The reaction is now drawable as a pose, not a face.
2. A silent false-hope beat makes the twist work.
3. The last line is understated, so the joke lands on the picture.
